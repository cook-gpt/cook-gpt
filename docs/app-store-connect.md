## Description

```
CookGPT — Gourmet Plan & Track helps you go from “what should we eat?” to a stocked kitchen and a clear cooking plan — all in one simple app.

Browse recipes, schedule your meals, and build a grocery list from what’s on your menu. Everything stays on your device. No account required.

RECIPES
• Save and organize your favorite recipes
• Filter by category — vegan, vegetarian, high-protein, quick, Italian, and more
• Sort by name, difficulty, or total time
• Scale ingredients for any number of servings
• Follow step-by-step instructions with built-in cooking timers
• Timers appear on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island while you cook
• Share recipes with friends and family
• Add your own recipes with custom ingredients and steps

MEALS
• View your schedule by day, week, or month
• Manually schedule lunch and dinner (and breakfast, if you enable it)
• Auto-plan a week of meals based on your diet type
• Favorites are prioritized when planning
• Optional breakfast planning in Settings

GROCERIES
• Generate a shopping list from scheduled meals or selected recipes
• Check off items as you shop
• See how many items you have left at a glance

SETTINGS
• Light, dark, or system theme
• Customize categories and ingredient units
• Reset to default data anytime

CookGPT — Gourmet Plan & Track is built for home cooks who want less mental load: plan once, shop once, cook with confidence.

Questions or feedback? We’d love to hear from you.
```
